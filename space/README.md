# 🇮🇳 Verbalyze: Indic Voice AI & Synthetic Data Suite

Welcome to the live interactive web demo of **Verbalyze**!

### Features
* 📞 **Interactive Telephony Voicebot**: Outbound loan recovery (Muthoot Fincorp), banking KYC, and food delivery simulated phone calls with voice speech and function calling (`disconnect_tool`, UPI links).
* 🎯 **Automated Human-Likeness Quality Gate**: Real-time acoustic scoring (cadence, breath phrasing, prosodic inflection, harmonic smoothness, and signal integrity) enforcing an 80% MOS 4.0 acceptance gate with rejection safety.
* 🏆 **Indic STT Benchmark Arena**: Side-by-side benchmark comparison of Whisper vs. Sarvam AI vs. Google Speech-to-Text across 11 difficult speech scenarios (Hinglish code-mixing, spoken numbers, acronyms, and noise).
* 📊 **Dataset Explorer**: Turn-by-turn viewer for the 16,370 multi-turn dialogues and 172,800 STT benchmark samples across 12 regional Indian languages.
